# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An international team presenting in English and Japanese in person. Presenters use Windows, macOS, and Linux, switch between slide tools and live demos, and use mirrored or extended projector displays. Audience members read translated captions while following the presentation.

## Product Purpose

Make presentations understandable across languages with a floating caption window, shared team vocabulary, and simple shared-code access. Preserve the meeting transcript separately from the short audience reading window, then help the presenter review the meeting.

## Brand Personality

Preserve the existing calm, clear, practical Stage interface. Controls should describe what they do in ordinary language.

## Anti-references

Avoid moving or constantly rewritten text that interrupts reading, account-heavy onboarding, and preview demonstrations presented as working screen capture. Preserve the current visual identity rather than redesigning the application.

## Design Principles

- Give the audience time to read without letting captions drift far behind the speaker.
- Keep provisional recognition separate from the audience's stable reading surface.
- Keep the complete transcript independently of caption clearing, display limits, or reconnections.
- Make recording, saved history, and sharing explicit.
- Keep credentials with the service that owns them and keep setup short for presenters.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support English and Japanese text, mixed-language terminology, keyboard controls, adjustable caption size, stable positioning, and reduced motion. Maintain readable contrast on the projected output and check the actual projector and fullscreen presentation applications.
